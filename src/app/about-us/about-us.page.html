Our Story & Mission

Our Coffee Shop, Our Project, Our Teamwork

Brewluxe is an artisanal coffee shop web application developed by 3rd-year Information Technology students from Technological Institute of the Philippines - Quezon City. We fused real-world web development with the comforting warmth of local café culture.

Our Purpose

To design an intuitive, mouth-watering ordering experience that feels like a real boutique café while refining our full-stack engineering skills.

Our Goal

To build a fast, responsive, and accessible digital platform that demonstrates teamwork, creative design, and production-grade software standards.

How We Built It

We collaborated across UI design, Angular/Ionic architecture, live weather integrations, address geolocation, and secure payment sandbox testing.
